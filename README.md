One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today — the warmest, most surprising line takes the $5.

## Entry

> My neighbour's cat, who ignored me for two years, picked this dawn to walk in, sleep on my keyboard, and purr through my whole deadline.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
